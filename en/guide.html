VPT39 User Guide

Video playback + projection mapping for live shows / Detailed edition

About this app:A video player made for projection mapping.
It is designed to be easy to use for media and interactive art with projectors, live shows and more.
VPT39 is an unofficial tool inspired by VPT (VideoProjectionTool) by HC Gilje. It is not affiliated with VPT in any way.

This guide helps first-time users of VPT39 work on their own, from preparation to the show and through any trouble. It explains the real screens with numbered marks.
During the show, also use the "Quick Start (A4, two-sided)". For the big picture, see "How It Works (A4, 2 pages, concept.pdf)".

1What VPT39 can do

VPT39 is an app for showing videos and photos on a projector and fitting them to the shape of a screen or wall, with just one laptop. It combines the "four-corner mapping" of VPT (VideoProjectionTool) with the VJ-app style of "lining up clips and switching between them".

FeatureWhat it does
Clip playbackLine up videos and images as thumbnails and click to switch. The picture changes smoothly from the previous one (crossfade)
Play columnSwitch the clips with the same number on all layers at once. Change all the pictures for each song together
LayersStack as many pictures as you like. Choose the blend mode (Normal, Add, Screen, Multiply) and the opacity
MappingMove the four corners of each layer to fit a trapezoid or tilted screen exactly
Multiple outputsUse any number of projectors. Choose which projector each layer shows on (also the same picture on several at once)
Wide screenJoin two or more projectors into one wide picture. The overlap is blended softly
Live inputsShow cameras, capture boards (another PC's screen and sound) or windows on the same PC (Zoom etc.)
Layer pictureShow one layer's picture at the same time in another place or on another output
Audio mixerPlay the sound of all videos at once. Set volume and mute per output and per layer
Photo wallShow the photos in a folder one after another. New photos are shown first
Auto saveSettings are saved automatically 2 seconds after a change. The next start begins from the same state

What you need

  • A Windows laptop (with graphics that support OpenGL 3.3)
  • A projector and a cable to the PC (HDMI etc.). One per projector if you use two or more
  • Speakers (for sound), from the PC's headphone jack, HDMI or an audio interface
  • You control VPT39 in a browser on the PC (Edge or Chrome)

Two screens

When VPT39 starts, two different screens open. Knowing the difference is the most important thing.

ScreenRoleWhere to put it
Output windowThe picture shown by the projector. You basically do not operate here (only fine-tuning the mapping)On the projector's display, fullscreen
Control panelThe control screen in the browser. Playback, volume and settings are all hereOn your laptop's own screen

  2. On the first screen, check "Add python.exe to PATH" at the bottom → "Install Now"
    If you forget this check, setup.bat cannot find Python. This is the most important part.
  1. If "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?" appears, choose "Yes"
  2. When "Setup was successful" appears, press "Close"
    You may or may not press "Disable path length limit", which appears on the way.

② Install VLC

  1. Open https://www.videolan.org/vlc/ and click the orange "Download VLC"
    On a Windows PC, the 64-bit version is chosen automatically.
  2. Run the downloaded "vlc-xx-win64.exe"
    Choose your language → keep pressing "Next" → "Install" → "Finish". You do not need to change any settings.
Without VLCVideos play but there is no sound. If the top left of the control panel shows "VLC (audio)", all is well. "OpenCV (no audio)" means VLC is not installed.

③ Extract the ZIP

  1. Right-click VPT39_配布用.zip ("for distribution") → "Extract All" → "Extract"
    Put it anywhere you like, such as the desktop or Documents. A VPT39 folder is created inside.
Do not open it from inside the ZIPIf you double-click the ZIP and run setup.bat while looking inside it, it will not work. Always "Extract All" first, then run it in the extracted folder.

④ Run setup.bat

    A black window opens and goes through [1/4] to [4/4]. It downloads components, so it takes 2 to 5 minutes. While text is scrolling, wait and do not close it.
  2. When "Success" appears, press any key to close it
If a blue warning appearsFor "Windows protected your PC", choose "More info" → "Run anyway". For "The publisher could not be verified", press "Run".

3Starting up and the screens

Double-click run.bat, and a black output window and the control panel in the browser open. If the control panel does not open, open http://localhost:8080/ in the browser.

  • 1Top bar: Brightness, BLACKOUT, Edit mapping, Phone, Setup, Save, Quit (Chapters 12 and 13)
  • 2Play column: plays the clips in the column right below on all layers at once (Chapter 7)
  • 3Layer rows: one row is one layer. Name and basic controls on the left, clips on the right. Lower rows are stacked in front (Chapter 6). With many layers, only this area scrolls
  • 4Add layer (Chapter 6)
  • 5Tabs: switch between "Media", "Layer settings", "Mixer" and "Outputs". ⧉ opens a separate window (below)
  • 6The content of the selected tab (here "Media": Chapter 5)

Top bar

  • 1VPT39 and status: the playback engine ("VLC (audio)" is normal), number of outputs, drawing speed (fps), and whether it is "unsaved"
  • 2Brightness: brightness of all outputs. It does not affect the volume
  • 3BLACKOUT (B): darkens all pictures in 0.5 seconds (the button glows yellow while dark). Press again to return. The sound does not stop
  • 4Clear all: fades out and clears all layers
  • 5Edit mapping (E): the mode for adjusting the corners (red while on). Always off during the show
  • 6Phone: adjust the corners from a phone (on/off and QR code, Chapter 9). Green "Phone ON" while on
  • 7Setup: (name): setup files for each venue or show (Chapter 13)
  • 8Save (save now) and Quit (after confirming, saves and closes the app)
  • 9日本語 / EN: switches the control panel and phone page between Japanese and English (remembered on this PC)

Tabs on the right and separate windows

The right side has four tabs: Media (Chapters 5 and 8) / Layer settings (Chapters 6 and 9) / Mixer (Chapter 11) / Outputs (Chapter 4).

  • Press a tab's ⧉, or drag the tab out of the browser window and drop it, and that panel becomes a separate window (it opens where you drop it). With many media files, a large separate Media window is handy
  • The tab of a panel in a separate window gets a "↗" and turns pale. Press it to bring that window to the front
  • Close the separate window (or press "Back to main screen") to put it back as a tab

The "selected layer"

Click a layer row and that layer becomes selected; the left edge of the row turns orange. The "Layer settings" tab, the mapping diagram and clicked media all apply to this selected layer.

4Showing on a projector (output windows)

  1. Connect the PC and the projector, and set the display to "Extend"
    Press ⊞Win+P and choose "Extend". With "Duplicate", your own screen is shown as it is.
  2. Find out which display is the projector
    Press Identify under "Display layout" in the "Outputs" tab. A number appears in the middle of each display for 4 seconds.
  3. Drag the output window to the projector's display
    Grab the bar at the top (title) of the small black window and move it to the projector side.
  4. Make it fullscreen
    Press Fullscreen in the "Outputs" tab, and it fills the display the window is on. Pressing F or double-clicking on the output window does the same.
  5. Check with your eyes that it appears on the screen. Keep the control panel on your own PC screen.
  • 1+ Add output: add Output 2, Output 3… (no limit)
  • 2Output name, resolution and current display (display 1 is the main one)
  • 3Fullscreen: on/off with each press. When on: no frame, always on top, no mouse cursor
  • 4Bring back: back to a window in the middle of the main display (if you lose it)
  • 5✕: delete that output (with two or more outputs; its layers move to Output 1)
  • 6Layers shown: check the layers to show on this output. Grayed out = "this is the layer's only output"
  • 7Name and size of the joined wide screen
  • 8Join (wide) check, Overlap, and the Gamma of that seam (below)
  • 9Identify / Display settings… (opens the Windows settings)
  • 10Display layout: order, resolution and scale of the connected displays. Green frame = a display with an output window
  • 11Show resolution: the resolution of the show projector (below)

With two or more projectors

There are two ways to use them.

UseHow
A different picture on eachAdd Output 2 with + Add output and make it fullscreen on the second display. Choose the layers with "Layers shown" (⑥) or with "Outputs" in the "Layer settings" tab
The same picture on bothCheck both outputs for one layer. The same picture appears at the same time. The corners are set per output (Chapter 9). The sound plays only once
One wide picture with twoSee "Wide screen" below

Wide screen (one picture with two projectors)

  1. Prepare two outputs. Make Output 1 (Output 2 in the example) fullscreen on the left projector and Output 2 (Output 3 in the example) on the right
  2. On the right output, check "Join to the right of Output ○ (wide)" (⑧). The two become one wide screen (e.g. 1920 + 1920), named "Output 2+Output 3 (wide)"
  3. Show the layer on this wide screen and fit its corners to the whole wide screen
  4. If the projector pictures overlap a little, enter the overlap width (px) in "Overlap". Both show the same picture in the overlap, blended smoothly
  5. If the overlap is brighter or darker than the rest, adjust "Gamma" (⑧) on that output's row (too bright → larger, too dark → smaller; normally 2.2). Gamma is per seam, so with three or more projectors you can set each seam
TipsUse the same resolution on both projectors. You can join three or more (Output 4 "to the right of Output 3"). A joined right-side output has no "Layers shown".
It remembersAt the next start, it opens fullscreen on the display with the same position and resolution. If that display is missing, it opens as a window with a warning.
Show resolution (⑪)Choose the show projector's resolution (1920×1080 etc.) and it always draws at that size. Adjust on another PC in a small window and use it as it is in the show. "Auto" draws at the window size.
× does not close it / scale 100%× does not close an output window (use ✕ in the "Outputs" tab). Set the projector display's Windows "Scale" to 100%.

Blank clips (show nothing)

  • 1Blank clip: press it, and the current picture and sound fade out; the layer shows nothing
  • 2+ Add blank: adds a blank clip at the end. You can also drag media onto this box

Use it when "the other layers are playing, but this layer should rest for this song". Like normal clips, it works with reordering, "Next" and "Play column", so it is also handy for filling gaps when you line up columns for each song.

Copying in from the PC / deleting

Copying in from the PCDrag and drop videos or images from File Explorer onto the "Media" area of the control panel, and they are copied to the media folder (drop when the green dotted line appears; any number at once). If a folder filter is on, they go into that folder. If the same name exists, it is saved as "name (2)". Files that are not videos or images are skipped. To delete media, put the mouse on the tile and press ✕ at the top right (moves to the Recycle Bin).

Reordering and copying

  • Drag a clip thumbnail to reorder it within the same layer (a yellow line shows where it will drop)
  • Drag it to another layer to copy it there ("At the end" goes back to "Loop")
  • Line up the columns for each song: put song 1's picture first on every layer, song 2's second, and so on. Then one "Play column" switches all layers. Put a blank on layers that show nothing

6Layers

A layer is "a sheet for stacking pictures". L1 is at the back; higher numbers are stacked further in front. There are two kinds of layers: video layers (play clips in a row) and photo walls (Chapter 10).

  • 1Name and number: in "L2 Video", L2 is the second layer from the back and "Video" is the kind
  • 2Now playing: file name, elapsed / length, progress
  • 3◀ Previous / Next ▶ / Clear (Chapter 7)
  • 4Show (shown while green; press for red "Hidden"), M (mute; red while muted), ▲▼ (stacking order), 🗑 (delete; asks first). While "Hidden", the sound keeps playing
  • 5Opacity
  • 6Clips (Chapters 5 and 7)

The "Layer settings" tab

  • 1Name
  • 2Opacity (same as on the left of the row)
  • 3Blend mode (table below)
  • 4Outputs: which outputs (projectors) show it. Check more than one to show the same picture at the same time. Hidden when there is only one output
  • 5Fade: seconds for a change (0–30 s). 0 is instant. The sound changes over the same time
  • 6Edge feather: softens the edges of the picture so spill-over is less visible (to join two projectors, use "Overlap" in Chapter 4)
  • 7Mapping (Chapter 9)

Mini controls (under the thumbnail)

How switching works (crossfade)

When you press a clip, the previous picture fades out while the next one fades in. The sound changes at the same time. The time it takes is the layer's "Fade".

No gapsUntil the first frame of the next video is ready (up to about 3 seconds), the previous video's picture and sound keep playing. If a video file is missing or broken, it does not switch; the previous picture stays, and red text on the left of the row tells you.

Layer picture (the same picture in another place or output)

Use the current picture itself of one layer as media on another layer. When you switch clips on the source layer, this one switches at the same time.

  • Example: show the picture of L1 "Background" on Output 2 as well, with L3 (L3 can have a different position and shape from L1)
  • It works even with "Show" off on the source layer (used only as a source)
  • The sound plays from the source layer (it does not double). A layer cannot show itself
  • To simply show "the same layer on two outputs", it is easier to check both in "Outputs" (Chapter 6)

Cameras and capture boards (another PC's screen and sound)

  1. Connect a capture board (HDMI→USB etc.) or camera to the PC and press "Refresh list"
  2. Add the device from the list to a layer and press the clip; the picture appears (delay about 0.1–0.2 s)
  3. For sound too, choose the audio input in the clip's "No audio" box (for a capture board, usually something like "Digital Audio Interface" with the same name). Set the volume on that layer in the mixer
TipsIf you change the audio input during playback, only the sound changes; the picture keeps going. Sound comes out of the Windows "default speaker". The OBS virtual camera also appears in the list, so you can bring in scenes made in OBS.
Two of the same modelWith two capture boards of the same model, the second shows as "name (#2)". Unplugging or restarting can swap the first and second, so always use the same USB ports.
If it is unpluggedIf a camera's cable comes out or its power goes off, the last picture stays, red text "The camera picture has stopped" appears on the left of the row, and it reconnects every 5 seconds automatically. The red text goes away when it reconnects.
One camera, one placeThe same camera cannot be opened by two clips at once (red text tells you). To show the same camera in another place, use "Layer picture".

Window capture (another app on the same PC)

You can capture the picture of any window, such as Zoom, Discord or a browser. Sound is not captured.

  • Do not minimize the window (it may stop). It still works when hidden behind other windows
  • For apps whose title changes (e.g. showing the current song), it looks for a window with a similar title
  • Protected video, such as streaming services, may turn black

Spout (receiving from other video apps)

Spout is a way for Windows apps to pass pictures to each other (like Syphon on Mac). It passes the picture directly inside the graphics card, so there is almost no delay, and transparent parts stay transparent when stacked. The sending app must support Spout.

Sending appHow to send with Spout
ProcessingThe "Spout for Processing" library (details below)
TouchDesignerConnect a "Syphon Spout Out" TOP after the TOP you want to send. The name is "Sender Name"
OBS StudioInstall the add-on "Spout2 Plugin for OBS", then start output in Tools → Spout Output Settings
VJ appsIf it supports Spout, turn on Spout in its output settings
  1. Turn on Spout output in the sending app (keep the app running)
  2. In VPT39: "Media" tab → Blank, layers, inputs → Refresh list
  3. The sender's name appears under "Spout". Drag it onto a layer row (or click to add). Press the clip to show it
If it stopsIf the sending app stops or closes, the last picture stays and red text "The Spout picture has stopped" appears. Send again with the same name and it continues automatically.
Use Python 3.13The Spout component supports Python only up to 3.13. If you run setup.bat with Python 3.14 or later, it shows "(Note) Spout does not work with this Python" and the Spout list stays empty (other features work).

Showing pictures made in Processing (Spout for Processing)

  1. Install the library: in Processing, menu "Sketch" → "Import Library" → "Add Library…". Type Spout in the search box, choose "Spout for Processing" and press "Install". Only once
  2. Add three things to the sketch (example below): ① import spout.*; at the very top ② create Spout and give it a name in setup() ③ spout.sendTexture(); at the end of draw()
  3. Use P2D or P3D in size(). Plain size(1280, 720) (JAVA2D) cannot send
  4. Run it (▶). Press "Refresh list" in VPT39, and the name you gave (e.g. Processing) appears under Spout
// ① Use Spout
import spout.*;
Spout spout;

void setup() {
  size(1280, 720, P2D);            // P2D or P3D (OpenGL)
  spout = new Spout(this);         // ② create it
  spout.setSenderName("Processing"); //    the name shown in VPT39's list
}

void draw() {
  background(0);
  fill(255, 120, 200);
  ellipse(mouseX, mouseY, 160, 160);
  spout.sendTexture();             // ③ send what was just drawn (end of draw)
}
To do thisWrite this
Transparent backgroundclear(); instead of background(0);. Only what you draw is stacked on top in VPT39 (blend mode "Normal" in VPT39)
ResolutionThe size() is sent as it is. Use the projection size (e.g. 1920, 1080) for a sharp picture
Send two or moreGive each sketch a different name (with the same name, only the one started last is used)
StoppingWhen you stop the sketch, VPT39 keeps the last picture and shows red text
Not in the list?Check: ① the sketch is running ② size() uses P2D/P3D ③ sendTexture() is called in draw() ④ you pressed "Refresh list". If it does not show on a laptop, go to Windows "Settings → Display → Graphics" and set both Processing (java.exe) and VPT39 (python.exe) to "High performance", so they run on the same graphics card.
On MacProcessing on Mac has a "Syphon" library used the same way. VPT39 is Windows only, so it receives Spout.

9Mapping (fitting the shape)

When a projector shines from an angle, the picture's shape is off. Move the four corners of each layer to fit the screen.

  1. Click a layer row to select it (the left edge turns orange). Open the "Layer settings" tab
  2. Turn on Edit mapping (E) in the top bar (or E). The output window shows the frame, the corners and a help line. Test pattern shows a grid to make distortion easy to see
  3. Drag the corners in the diagram to the screen's corners (drag inside to move it all)
  4. Finally, fine-tune with the arrow keys: choose a corner (or all) in ⑥ and move 1 px at a time. In front of the screen, use a phone (next page)

Adjusting with a phone (setup mode)

You can leave the PC and adjust the corners with a phone in front of the screen or in the audience. While you use the phone, a yellow frame and a red mark (the part you are moving) appear in the picture on the screen itself, so you can keep watching the screen and just press the arrow pad.

  • 1Show frame: shows the frame and mark on the screen (same as "Edit mapping" on the PC). Turns on automatically when opened on the phone. Always turn it off when done. A green dot at the top left means connected
  • 2Choose the Layer, and the output if it is shown on several (the PC's selection changes too)
  • 3Diagram: drag a corner with your finger to reshape, drag inside to move (for rough positioning)
  • 4What to move: Top left, Top right, Bottom left, Bottom right, All (green = selected)
  • 5Arrow pad: tap to move once, hold to keep moving (faster after 1.5 s). The middle button switches 1 px / 10 px
  • 6Undo (a held move counts as one), Test pattern (grid; red while on), Reset corners (asks first; Undo brings it back)
  • 7Opacity and Feather

How to connect (QR code)

  1. Press "Phone" in the top bar, then "Control from a phone" (①). No restart needed. The first time, if a "Windows Security Alert" appears, check "Private networks" and press "Allow access"
  2. Connect the PC and the phone to the same Wi-Fi, and scan the QR code (②) with the phone camera. The adjustment page opens (the address is also shown below, so you can type it)
  3. When done, press "Stop phone control". On/off is saved and stays the same at the next start. After changing the Wi-Fi, press "Find the address again" (③)
Be carefulThere is no password, so anyone on the same Wi-Fi can control it. Use your own router or phone tethering. Venue and hotel Wi-Fi often blocks connections between devices, so it may not connect. When not in use, press "Stop phone control".
Keep the screen onSet the phone's "Auto-Lock" longer (e.g. 5 minutes). If the connection is lost, red "Not connected to the PC" appears at the top of the phone and the buttons stop working (they come back when it reconnects).

10Photo wall

A layer that shows the photos in a chosen folder one after another. Use it to show photos taken at the venue or collected from the audience.

  • 1The photo wall row (name, "Show" etc.; it has no sound, so no M)
  • 2Images in the row: press one to show it now (green frame = shown now). Drag to reorder, ✕ to remove
  • 3Drag images here from the media to line them up
  • 4Number of photos, interval, and Next photo ▶ (next one now)

Operating notesThe photo wall shows every photo in its folder. Choose a folder that only gets photos a person has checked and approved (e.g. an "approved" folder in Dropbox). Also take care to get consent from the people in the photos.

11Audio mixer

The sound of video layers and cameras all plays at the same time, whichever output they are on. Set the volume in the "Mixer" tab.

  • 1Audio master: overall volume
  • 2Per output: volume of all layers shown on that output (a layer on several outputs is under its first output)
  • 3Per layer: that layer's volume. The green bar below shows roughly how loud it is playing (it moves during a crossfade)
  • 4M: Mute. Red means muted

The actual volume is layer × output × audio master. If any of them is 0 or M (mute), that sound is silent. The photo wall has no sound, so it is not in the mixer. Layer picture, window and blank clips make no sound.

Sound that does not stopBLACKOUT or "Show" off does not stop the sound (so music does not cut out during a live show). To stop sound, use M or the fader.

No sound? (check in order)

  1. Is the PC volume (speaker in the taskbar) at 0 or muted?
  2. Is the Windows sound output set to the connected speakers?
  3. Is an M in the mixer red, or a fader all the way left?
  4. Does the top left of the control panel say "VLC (audio)"? (If it says "one volume for all layers", per-layer volume does not work)
  5. Does the video file itself have sound? For a camera, is the clip's audio input set to "No audio"?

If the app crashes

  1. Use AV mute (picture mute) on the projector's remote so the audience does not see the desktop
  2. Double-click run.bat
  3. Wait until all outputs open fullscreen on their displays (for any that do not, follow Chapter 4)
  4. Press the clips again (order, shape and volume are saved; the playback position is not)
  5. Check the picture and sound, then turn off AV mute
If only the control panel was closedThe picture has not stopped. Open http://localhost:8080/ in the browser to control it again as before.

13Saving and setup files

  • Settings are saved to project.json automatically 2 seconds after a change (and to the open setup file, if any). To save right away, press Save
  • Press Quit to save and close after confirming
  • Saved: layers and clips, corners, volumes, output windows. Not saved: the playback position, BLACKOUT and test pattern (off at start)

Setup (setup files for each venue or show)

For each venue or show, you can save the running order, clips, layers, mapping, volume and outputs together under a name. Press "Setup: (name)" in the top bar to open the window.

  • 1Save as: enter a name and save (Enter works too). If the name exists, it asks before overwriting
  • 2The setup open now (green frame). Changes are saved over it automatically 2 seconds later
  • 3Open: after confirming, switches to that setup
  • 4Duplicate: saves the open setup under a new name (to make a version for another venue)
  • 5✕: to the Recycle Bin (asks first; you can restore it)
  • Files are saved as "name.json" in the VPT39\settings folder. Sync it with Dropbox to use the same list on another PC
  • Opening a setup also recreates the output windows. On a PC without the saved displays, those outputs open as windows; move them to the projection display and make them fullscreen. Do not open one during the show (the picture cuts out for a moment)
  • Before opening, the current setup is saved ("(unnamed)" is replaced; use "Save as" first to keep it)
  • When you prepare on another PC, set each output's "Show resolution" (Chapter 4) to the show projector, and the corners and overlaps can be used as they are in the show

15Pre-show checklist

By the day before

  • Exported the media as H.264, 1080p, 30fps mp4
  • Put the media in the media folder; every clip plays
  • Lined up the columns (numbers) for each song
  • The photo wall folder only gets "approved" photos
  • Did a full rehearsal with the same PC and the same projectors as the show
  • Made a copy of project.json
  • Finished Windows Update and set it not to restart during the show

At the venue

  • Connected the PC's power cable (do not run on battery)
  • ⊞Win+P is "Extend"; projector scale is 100%
  • Outputs are fullscreen on the right displays (check with Identify)
  • Fitted the mapping (for a wide screen, also check the seam and overlap)
  • Checked the picture and sound of cameras, capture boards, captured windows and Spout
  • With the test media (below), checked smoothness, audio sync and left/right speakers

Right before the show

  • Edit mapping is off (the top bar button is not red; if you adjusted from a phone, "Show frame" is off too)
  • Test pattern is off (no grid on screen)
  • Checked BLACKOUT (on = glowing yellow, if it stays dark until the show starts)
  • The M buttons in the audio mixer are as intended; checked the volume
  • Heard sound from the speakers with your own ears
  • Notifications off (Windows "Do not disturb" / Focus)
  • Sleep and screen-off time set to "Never"
  • Closed other apps
  • Put the quick start next to the equipment
  • Know where AV mute is on the projector remote
